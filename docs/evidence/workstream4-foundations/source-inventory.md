## First pilot

Wellcome’s directory returned 10 entries. The public page’s embedded data
showed three “Apply anytime,” three “Open to applications” partner or
institution-run entries, and four “Closed to applications.” The general
scheme status was “Open” even on the four closed entries. A pilot must use
the actual application status and verify the issuer’s detail page.

Start with the three direct schemes below. Their public pages and Wellcome’s
deadline calendar confirm rolling submissions. Record each scheme’s own
administering-organization rules. For example, the Early-Career Award
specifies the UK and eligible low- and middle-income countries in Africa,
South Asia and South-East Asia.

- [Wellcome Early-Career Awards](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/schemes/wellcome-early-career-awards)
- [Wellcome Career Development Awards](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/schemes/wellcome-career-development-awards)
- [Wellcome Discovery Awards](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/schemes/wellcome-discovery-awards)

Run one local import before connecting it to the daily builder. Verify the
three records against their official pages, exercise one closed entry,
preserve rolling deadlines and source eligibility, and compare all candidate
records with the federal snapshot for duplicates. Check at a weekly cadence
for the pilot and attribute Wellcome in each normalized record. Leave
unstated values empty.
